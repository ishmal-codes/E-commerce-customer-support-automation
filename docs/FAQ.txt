FAQ
1. Can I modify or cancel my order after placing it?

Order changes or cancellations can be requested within 2 hours of purchase. After an order enters processing or has shipped, changes may no longer be possible. Contact customer support as soon as possible with your order number.

2. What payment methods do you accept?

We accept major credit and debit cards, including Visa and Mastercard, as well as Apple Pay and other payment methods displayed at checkout. Payment must be successfully authorized before an order is processed.

3. Do you offer international shipping?

Yes. We ship to selected international destinations. Standard International Shipping generally takes 7–14 business days, while Express International Shipping generally takes 3–7 business days after processing.

4. How much does shipping cost?

Domestic Standard Shipping costs $9.99 and Express Shipping costs $19.99. Orders over $500 receive free Standard Shipping. International Standard Shipping costs $29.99 and Express Shipping costs $59.99.

5. How can I track my order?

Once your order ships, we email you a tracking number and carrier information. You can use the tracking number on the carrier's website to view the latest delivery status.

6. What should I do if my iPhone arrives damaged?

Do not use or attempt to repair the device. Contact us within 48 hours of delivery and provide your order number and clear photos of the packaging and device. If the damage is confirmed, we will provide a replacement or refund and cover the return shipping cost.

7. What if my device is DOA or does not turn on?

Contact support within 48 hours of delivery. We may ask you to perform basic troubleshooting before approving a return. If the device is confirmed to be dead on arrival, we will arrange a replacement or refund.

8. What warranty coverage do you provide?

New Apple devices are covered by Apple's applicable limited warranty according to Apple's warranty terms. Warranty claims may be handled directly through Apple or with our support team, depending on the issue and applicable warranty coverage.

9. Are your products genuine Apple products?

Yes. Our demo catalog is intended to represent genuine Apple products and Apple accessories. Each device is identified by its product details and, where applicable, its IMEI or serial number.

10. Do you sell refurbished or used iPhones?

No. We do not sell refurbished or used iPhones in this store. Products listed as new are intended to be brand-new units.

11. Can I return an iPhone after activating it?

Yes, provided the return is requested within 14 calendar days and the device meets all return conditions. Before sending it back, you must sign out of your Apple ID and disable Find My iPhone.

12. Who pays for return shipping?

Customers pay return shipping for unwanted or incorrectly ordered products. We provide a prepaid return label when the item is defective, damaged during delivery, or incorrectly shipped.

13. When will I receive my refund?

After we receive and inspect the returned product, approved refunds are normally issued within 5–7 business days to the original payment method.

14. What happens if the product I want as an exchange is out of stock?

If the replacement model or variant is unavailable, you can choose another eligible product of similar value, receive a refund, or receive store credit valid for 12 months.

15. Are customs duties included in international shipping?

No. International customers are responsible for customs duties, import taxes, brokerage fees, and other charges imposed by the destination country's authorities.
