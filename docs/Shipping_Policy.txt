SHIPPING POLICY
1. Order Processing

Orders are processed within 1–2 business days after payment is confirmed. Orders placed after 2:00 PM local time, on weekends, or on public holidays begin processing on the next business day.

Once an order has shipped, customers receive a tracking number by email.

2. Domestic Shipping

Domestic orders are delivered within 3–5 business days after processing.

Standard Shipping: $9.99
Express Shipping: $19.99, delivered within 1–2 business days after processing
Orders over $500 qualify for free Standard Shipping.

Delivery times are estimates and may vary depending on the destination, courier, weather, or other circumstances outside our control.

3. International Shipping

International shipping is available to selected countries.

Standard International: $29.99, estimated delivery in 7–14 business days
Express International: $59.99, estimated delivery in 3–7 business days

International customers are responsible for customs duties, import taxes, brokerage fees, and other charges imposed by the destination country.

4. Delayed Shipments

If a domestic shipment has not arrived within 7 business days after the estimated delivery date, customers should contact support with their order number.

For international shipments, customers should contact support if the package has not arrived within 21 business days after the estimated delivery date.

We will investigate delayed shipments with the carrier. If the carrier confirms that a package is lost, we will provide a replacement when inventory is available or issue a full refund.
