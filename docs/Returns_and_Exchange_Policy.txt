RETURNS & EXCHANGE POLICY
1. Return Window

Customers may request a return within 14 calendar days of receiving their order.

Return requests submitted after 14 days are generally not eligible unless the product is covered by a separate warranty or arrives defective.

2. Return Conditions

To qualify for a return, an iPhone or accessory must:

Be in its original condition with no cracks, liquid damage, or significant physical damage.
Include the original box, accessories, manuals, and other included items.
Have all included accessories and components.
Not be reported as lost, stolen, or fraudulently obtained.
Be removed from the customer's Apple ID and have Find My iPhone disabled before shipment.

For smartphones, the device's IMEI must match the device originally purchased from our store.

Products returned with missing accessories or significant damage may receive a partial refund or be rejected.

3. Return Shipping

Customers are responsible for return shipping costs for unwanted or incorrectly ordered products.

If the product is defective, damaged during delivery, or the wrong product was shipped, we will provide a prepaid return shipping label.

4. Refunds

Approved returns are refunded to the original payment method after the returned product has been inspected.

Refunds are normally issued within 5–7 business days after the returned item passes inspection. Original shipping charges are non-refundable unless the return resulted from our error or the product arrived damaged or defective.

5. Exchanges

Customers may request an exchange for an eligible product within the 14-day return window, subject to inventory availability.

If the requested replacement is unavailable, customers may choose a refund or store credit.

Store credit is issued electronically and remains valid for 12 months from the date of issue.
